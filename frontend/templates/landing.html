How it works

Three steps between a sentence and a plan you can act on.

1

Describe the trip

Type it the way you’d tell a friend, or use the trip builder to fill in origin, dates, budget and interests.

2

Agents research in parallel

Dedicated agents look into flights, hotels and live weather at the same time, backed by real data providers.

3

Review your plan

Findings are assembled into a day-by-day itinerary with a budget estimate, split into tabs you can copy or export.

Frequently asked

How long does a plan take?

30–90 seconds. Several specialist agents research your trip in turn, and each step involves live data and AI calls.

Does it book anything for me?

No. TripSage researches and plans — flights, hotels, weather and the itinerary — so you can book directly with the providers it points you to.

Is my trip saved?

Yes. Every plan is saved as you go, so you can reopen it later and ask follow-up questions without starting over.

What does it cost?

TripSage itself is free and open-source. It runs on API keys you provide for the underlying AI and data providers, most of which have a free tier.
